## The three estimands

[Item 2](preregistration.md#2-primary-baseline-identity) declared one primary comparison before
any evaluation-half read: **M9, the full v0.4 guarded router, against the best fixed
configuration** chosen by `select_best_fixed` on the selection half and scored only on the
evaluation half. The three estimands are reported against that baseline —
`g_out` the oracle opportunity, `g_z` what a chooser seeing only the routing signal could
reach, `g_learn` what the router did — with Clopper–Pearson intervals at the
[item 13](preregistration.md#13-multiplicity-handling) level of α/(K + L) = 0.05/9, and the
paired regret contrast [item 5](preregistration.md#5-confidence-interval--bootstrap-procedure)
names as the primary analysis: a project-clustered bootstrap over within-task differences,
B = 2000, seeded from the corpus.

```text
# estimands-locked - locked corpus, EVALUATION half, M9 against the selection-valid best fixed configuration
estimand                                           value      interval at the adjusted level
-------------------------------------------------  ---------  ------------------------------
g_out (oracle opportunity)                         0.222222   [-0.396297, 0.710224]
g_z (signal-restricted opportunity)                -0.055556  [-0.507748, 0.429923]
g_learn (learned gain)                             0.166667   [-0.427536, 0.662614]
best fixed configuration (cnf-claude-sonnet-lean)  3/18       [0.015972, 0.512109]
paired regret reduction (M0 - M9)                  0.406954   [0.009603, 0.939517]

selection-half rate  2/18
adjusted alpha       0.005556 (Bonferroni over K = 6 configurations and L = 3 policies)
recovered fraction   not reported - the opportunity gap's lower limit is not positive
```

**What this says, and what it does not.**

- **The paired regret contrast excludes zero.** M9 regretted 0.406954 less per node than the
  fixed baseline, interval [0.009603, 0.939517] at α/9. This is item 5's primary analysis on
  item 2's primary comparison, and it is the one superiority statement this release makes.
- **The binary superiority claim is not made.** `g_learn` is 0.166667 with an interval that
  spans zero. Verified success at 18 evaluation tasks is a small-count endpoint; item 1 said so
  before the read (≈ 6,000 units would be needed at δ = 0.02) and reported it as a gate rather
  than as a powered comparison. It is reported here as measured and not as a finding.
- **No recovered fraction is quoted.** `g_out`'s lower limit is not positive, so the share of
  the opportunity the router recovered is not a small number — it is not a number, and
  `estimands` returns `None` rather than a ratio with an undefined sign. The confirmatory audit
  this analysis follows (*Opportunity is not realizability*,
  <https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2608.08265>) found routers recovering 7.5–14.4% of certified
  oracle opportunity; this corpus cannot distinguish that from zero at the adjusted level, and
  saying so is the result.

## The gates, apart from utility

The two safety criteria are the `verified_success_floor` (0.70) and `false_acceptance_ceiling`
(0.05) registered in every corpus's
[`config.v1.json`](../../../evals/router/locked/config.v1.json). They are computed from the
recorded verdicts alone and share no expression with the objective, so re-weighting utility
cannot move them, and `GateReport` and `RegretReport` are different types so that neither can be
computed from the other. They are in their own table for that reason.

```

**Every policy fails both gates on both corpora, and that is a property of the frozen size
rather than of the routers.** The corpus pools a cell's trials conservatively — a configuration
has *verified* a node only if it verified it on every trial, and one false acceptance among the
trials is a false acceptance — and those two rules were chosen when a cell held two trials. At
the frozen 18 they are close to degenerate: on the locked corpus the per-trial verified rate is
0.5121 and the pooled rate is 0.0870, while the per-trial false-acceptance rate is 0.0390 and
the pooled rate is 0.4348. The gate thresholds (floor 0.70, ceiling 0.05) were registered
against the per-trial scale and are being read on the pooled one.

This is recorded, not repaired. Changing the pooling rule after seeing these rows would be
exactly the post-hoc analysis change the pre-registration exists to prevent, and item 1 is
frozen. It is filed as `ADR4-M10-005` in
[the v0.4 backlog](../../releases/v0.4/backlog.md) and it is why the utility table below is
reported separately: the two columns disagree, and the disagreement is the finding.

## The ten registered ablations

The protocol's [§14 table](../../../evals/router/ablations.v1.json), reached through the
corpus's `ablations_path` and never by convention, so moving a file cannot repoint a run. Each
row removes exactly one component from the full router and re-runs M9 on the same 18 evaluation
tasks.

---  -------------------------  ------------  ---------  -----------  --------

A2   compatibility_pruning      0.610325      0.000000   0.054533     6/18
A3   experience_retrieval       0.603631      -0.006694  0.061226     3/18
A4   project_adapter            0.610325      0.000000   0.054533     6/18

A6   final_run_feedback         0.614442      0.004117   0.050416     6/18
A7   guarded_exploration        0.660700      0.050375   0.004158     6/18
A8   independent_verification   0.610538      0.000213   0.054320     6/18
A9   uncertainty_gate           0.618144      0.007820   0.046713     5/18
A10  evi_recovery_stop          0.500820      -0.109504  0.164037     3/18
```

The two components whose removal costs most are `evi_recovery_stop` (A10, −0.109504) and
`experience_retrieval` (A3, −0.006694). `guarded_exploration` (A7) *improves* mean utility by
0.050375 on this corpus, which is the honest reading of an exploration policy on a replay:
exploration pays for information the replay does not let it use, so its cost is visible and its
benefit is not. That is a limitation of replay evaluation, not evidence that the guard should
be removed.

## The priced real-provider run: **not run**

No v0.4 measurement anywhere in this release touched a real provider. Every number on this page
is a replay over committed synthetic traces, and `RouterBenchmarkRunner` refuses any
`execution_source` other than `REPLAY` at the route and again inside the runner. The question
is recorded rather than omitted: **the priced run was not performed, and nothing here is
evidence about hosted models.**

## Reproduce

```bash
ACCRETION_ROUTER_LOCKED_TEST=1 PYTHONPATH=src uv run --no-sync python \
  scripts/router_locked_test.py --principal usr-accretion-release \
  --reason "the v0.4.0 release's locked read (M10d)"
```

Running that command **is** a read of the locked test set: it appends a row to
[`access-log.jsonl`](access-log.jsonl) for each of the two corpora. To rehearse without touching
the committed log, pass `--access-log` a path outside the repository. To check the page without
reading anything through the door at all, run the regeneration test:

```bash
PYTEST_DISABLE_PLUGIN_AUTOLOAD=1 uv run --no-sync pytest -p pytest_asyncio.plugin \
  tests/test_v04_m10_locked_test.py tests/test_v04_m10_locked_corpus.py
```

The corpora themselves are regenerated with `python -m tests.router_corpus_generator`, which
must leave every committed file byte-identical.

## What this page does not do

It makes no claim about hosted models, quotes no number from the development corpus
`evals/router`, and reports no recovered fraction. It states one superiority result — the
paired regret contrast — at the multiplicity-adjusted level, on a corpus generated after the
analysis was frozen, replicated on a provider-drift holdout, with the read recorded. See the
[release audit](../../releases/v0.4/audit.md) for how that evidence is graded and the
[research index](../README.md) for where it sits beside the v0.1–v0.3 experiments.
