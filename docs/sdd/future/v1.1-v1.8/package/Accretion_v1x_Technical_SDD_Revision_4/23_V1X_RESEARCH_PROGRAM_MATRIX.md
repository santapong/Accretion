## 2. Cross-study invariants

- Freeze the estimand, task population, candidates, verifier, acquisition rule, cost ledger, stopping rule and analysis code before adaptive observations.
- Use project/domain/time separation appropriate to the claim. The final hidden set is accessed once.
- Count non-PASS outcomes at the frozen TTVS horizon and retain them in the success denominator.
- Keep platform readiness, research closure and artifact promotion as separate decisions.
- Preserve negative, contradictory and inconclusive evidence.
- Do not use later-release research to bypass an earlier operational gate.

## 3. Research priority

1. v1.1 trace inventory and verifier qualification.
2. v1.1 offline replay of uniform and adaptive acquisition.
3. v1.1 bounded three-profile development pilot, only after protocol freeze.
4. v1.1 common holdout and selection-regret report.
5. Reassess whether v1.2 is still the highest-value next release; later SDDs remain designs, not commitments to run.


## 4. Revision-4 stage refinements

| Release | First stage | Separately gated follow-up | Main falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| v1.1 | Actual-setting and complete-trace inventory; three fixed profiles | Conditional routing after fixed-profile and operational closure | Uniform acquisition matches or beats the candidate after full cost |
| v1.2 | Mature general/specialist factorial with activation evidence | Learned regime/joint selection | Gains disappear over a mature baseline or after maintenance cost |
| v1.3 | Localization and restorable-episode coverage | Repeated no-op/full-retry/intervention comparison | Stochastic reruns explain the gain or recovery costs more |
| v1.4 | Trust and dependency serialization | Delayed/cross-session poisoning and utility comparison | Stale consumption, attack harm or bookkeeping erases benefit |
| v1.5 | Frozen-candidate trace acquisition | Separate behavior-linked screen with fresh development confirmation | Selected repairs regress preservation/boundary cases or final test |
| v1.6 | Optional verified-SFT feasibility after simpler methods | Optional four-arm harness/adapter interaction | Total cost has no realistic break-even or joint pair fails |
| v1.7 | Context-only target/source/empty-prior comparison | Frozen/periodic/triggered updates with supported label timing | Negative transfer or labels cannot support the chosen analysis |
| v1.8 | Command-disabled workflow allocation with deterministic solver | Separate scenario calibration, then separately governed physical work | Solver wins, queue model fails, or intervals/target data are inadequate |

Follow-up applicability must be decided and frozen before outcomes are observed. An optional study can be explicitly deferred with rationale and revisit trigger; its criteria stay pending/deferred and are never marked PASS. No follow-up can waive a mandatory release gate or unlock physical execution. The new data-role and comparison records are required for whichever study is actually launched.
